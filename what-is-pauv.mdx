**Trade on people.**

Pauv is the world's first real-time index of public sentiment. We host the **Net Public Sentiment Index (NPSI)**: a live, dollar-denominated measure of how culturally relevant a public figure is — their **Sentiment** — shaped entirely by trading activity on the platform.

Users trade **up** or **down** on a public figure's Sentiment based on whether they believe that person's cultural relevance will rise or fall. Every trade settles instantly through Pauv's proprietary pricing engine, **VBC 4.1 (Valence Bonding Curve)**: no counterparty, no binary outcome, no expiration.

Pauv is not a stock exchange, a prediction market, a meme coin, or an NFT project. It is a **signal market**: a continuous, transparent measure of public perception.
