## Core Concepts

- **NPSI (Net Public Sentiment Index)**: A live, dollar-denominated measure of a public figure's cultural relevance. Every public figure on Pauv has one. The NPSI is shaped entirely by forecasting activity.
- **Positive Forecast**: A long position on the NPSI, indicating the user believes the figure's cultural relevance will rise. Pulls the NPSI up.
- **Negative Forecast**: An escrow-backed short position on the NPSI, indicating the user believes the figure's cultural relevance will fall. Pulls the NPSI down. Automatically closed if the NPSI rises enough that the buy-back cost reaches the liquidation threshold of the position's escrow.
- **DTM 4.1**: Pauv's proprietary pricing engine. A single continuous bonding curve with a softplus price floor, driven in opposite directions by Positive and Negative Forecasts. Settles every forecast instantly with no counterparty.
- **Good-faith royalty**: 0.5% of every forecast transaction, set aside for the public figure whose NPSI is being forecasted. Claimable to a verified Solana wallet, donatable to a verified 501(c)(3), or escheated to the state.
- **Balance**: Your on-platform USD-denominated balance, funded by depositing **USDC on Solana** from your connected wallet. Withdraw back to your wallet at any time.

## Key Features

- **Continuous signal**: The NPSI is live and always on. It has a floor at zero and no ceiling, moving in real time as users open and close forecasts.
- **Instant settlement**: Every forecast is priced and settled instantly by DTM 4.1. No order book, no counterparty matching, no liquidity requirements.
- **Two-sided expression**: Users can forecast that cultural relevance will rise (Positive) or fall (Negative). Both directions drive the same curve in opposite directions, producing a self-correcting signal.
- **Wallet-native funding**: Connect a Solana wallet via browser extension or QR code, deposit USDC, and start forecasting. No bank account, no card on file.
- **Non-transferable positions**: Your on-platform balance is used to open and close forecasts. Forecasts and positions cannot be transferred to other users.

## What Pauv Is Not

Pauv occupies a category it calls the **signal market**, distinct from any other market structure:

| Pauv Is Not | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| A stock exchange or securities market | Forecasts are not ownership, equity, debt, or investment contracts. |
| A prediction market | There are no binary outcomes, no event-based settlement, and no expiration. |
| A meme coin platform or NFT marketplace | NPSI positions are internal records and are not transferable on-chain. |
| A crowdfunding or creator monetization tool | Users are not funding projects. Forecasts measure perception, not intent. |

Pauv's official industry category is **Technology, Information, and Internet**.
