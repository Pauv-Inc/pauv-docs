## Core Concepts

- **NPSI (Net Public Sentiment Index)**: A live, dollar-denominated measure of a public figure's cultural relevance. Every public figure on Pauv has one. In everyday use we just call it a person's **Sentiment**. Once a profile's starting value is set, the NPSI is shaped entirely by trading activity.
- **Trading up and down**: You can go **long** (you believe a figure's cultural relevance will rise — pushes the NPSI up) or **short** (you believe it will fall — pushes the NPSI down). Shorts are escrow-backed and are automatically liquidated if the cost to buy back the position reaches 95% of its escrow.
- **VBC 4.1 (Valence Bonding Curve)**: Pauv's proprietary pricing engine. A single continuous bonding curve with a softplus price floor, driven in opposite directions by long and short trades. Settles every trade instantly with no counterparty.
- **Balance**: Your USD balance, funded through Glide and credited 1:1 with your deposits. Withdraw at any time.

## Key Features

- **Continuous signal**: The NPSI is live and always on. It has a floor at zero and no ceiling, moving in real time as users open and close positions.
- **Instant settlement**: Every trade is priced and settled instantly by VBC 4.1. No order book, no counterparty matching, no liquidity requirements.
- **Long and short**: Trade up if you think cultural relevance will rise, or down if you think it will fall. Both directions drive the same curve in opposite directions, producing a self-correcting signal.
- **Simple funding**: Deposit through **Glide** and your balance is credited in U.S. dollars. Withdraw at any time, automatically.
- **Referral program**: Refer a friend and collect **100% of the trading fees on their first 100 trades**, credited to your balance in real time. See [Referral Program](/platform/referrals).
- **Auto-Spread**: By default, 95% of an order goes to the person you selected and 5% is spread across that profile's industry or subindustry. Adjust the split up to 70/30, or turn it off. See [Trading](/platform/trading#auto-spread).
- **Non-transferable positions**: Your balance is used to open and close positions. Positions cannot be transferred to other users.

## What Pauv Is Not

Pauv occupies a category it calls the **signal market**, distinct from any other market structure:

| Pauv Is Not | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| A stock exchange or securities market | Trades are not ownership, equity, debt, or investment contracts. |
| A prediction market | There are no binary outcomes, no event-based settlement, and no expiration. |
| A meme coin platform or NFT marketplace | NPSI positions are internal records and are not transferable on-chain. |
| A crowdfunding or creator monetization tool | Users are not funding projects. Trades measure perception, not intent. |

Pauv's official industry category is **Technology, Information, and Internet**.
