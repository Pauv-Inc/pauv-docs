## Priority Escalations

Inquiries related to the following are treated as **Priority Escalations** and triaged within 12 business hours:

- Unauthorized account access or suspected fraud
- Account lockouts
- Deposit not credited after on-chain confirmation
- Withdrawal review questions
- Account security concerns

## Reporting Fraud or Unauthorized Access

If you suspect unauthorized access to your **Pauv account**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email support immediately">
    Send an email to **support@pauv.com** with **"URGENT: ACCOUNT SECURITY"** or **"URGENT: FRAUD"** in the subject line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account freeze">
    Upon receipt, your account and on-platform balance will be **temporarily frozen** to prevent unauthorized activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit & withdrawal suspension">
    All deposit and withdrawal capabilities for the affected account are suspended during investigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account recovery">
    Our team will work with you to secure your account and resume normal access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Pauv cannot recover funds lost due to a compromised wallet. Protect your wallet's recovery phrase the way you would protect the keys to your bank account.
</Warning>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do withdrawals take?">
    During the beta period, every withdrawal request is reviewed by our team and approved within **12 hours**. The only reason a withdrawal would be denied is a genuine flaw or error — for example, the trading engine credited you more than it should have. Once approved, USDC is sent to your connected Solana wallet on-chain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my withdrawal denied?">
    Withdrawals are only denied if our review finds a genuine flaw or error in how your balance was calculated. If that happens, we will contact you to explain what we found and how we will resolve it. This safeguard exists during beta while we ensure the stability of our trading engine.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My deposit hasn't shown up yet. What do I do?">
    Confirm the transaction was sent as **USDC on Solana** to your Pauv deposit address. Once the deposit is confirmed on-chain, your balance is credited automatically. If you sent the wrong token or used a different network, contact support — recovery may not be possible.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my balance or forecasts to someone else?">
    No. On-platform balances and positions are not transferable to other users. To move funds, withdraw to your own wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the NPSI moves against my forecast?">
    The NPSI is driven entirely by forecasting activity through DTM 4.1. If forecasting pushes the index against you, closing your forecast will return less than you committed. A Negative Forecast can be automatically liquidated at zero return if the NPSI rises enough to consume its liquidation threshold of the escrow. Pauv does not offer refunds for NPSI movement or liquidations. See our [Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm a public figure. How do I claim my NPSI profile?">
    Visit your NPSI profile on pauv.com and select **Claim Your Profile**. Our team will verify your identity and you can choose to direct your good-faith royalty to a verified Solana wallet (paid in USDC) or to a verified 501(c)(3) charity. See [For Public Figures](/platform/for-talent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a public figure listed?">
    Anyone can submit a public figure to be listed, and public figures (or their authorized representatives) can request their own listing. Either way, Pauv reviews and approves before the profile goes live. See [For Public Figures](/platform/for-talent).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
