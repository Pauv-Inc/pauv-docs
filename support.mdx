## Priority Escalations

Inquiries related to the following are treated as **Priority Escalations** and triaged within 12 business hours:

- Unauthorized account access or suspected fraud
- Account lockouts
- Deposit not credited
- Withdrawal questions
- Account security concerns

## Reporting Fraud or Unauthorized Access

If you suspect unauthorized access to your **Pauv account**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Email support immediately">
    Send an email to **support@pauv.com** with **"URGENT: ACCOUNT SECURITY"** or **"URGENT: FRAUD"** in the subject line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account freeze">
    Upon receipt, your account and balance will be **temporarily frozen** to prevent unauthorized activity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit & withdrawal suspension">
    All deposit and withdrawal capabilities for the affected account are suspended during investigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Account recovery">
    Our team will work with you to secure your account and resume normal access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long do withdrawals take?">
    Withdrawals are automatic. Press withdraw and it processes with no manual review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My deposit hasn't shown up yet. What do I do?">
    Deposits through Glide are credited automatically. If a completed deposit has not been credited, contact support with your Glide transaction reference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I transfer my balance or positions to someone else?">
    No. Balances and positions are not transferable to other users. To move funds, withdraw from your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if the NPSI moves against my trade?">
    The NPSI is driven entirely by trading activity through VBC 4.1. If the market moves against you, closing your position will return less than you committed. A short is automatically liquidated if the NPSI rises enough that the buy-back cost reaches 95% of the position's escrow; whatever remains of the escrow after the buy-back is returned to you. Pauv does not offer refunds for NPSI movement or liquidations. See our [Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How does the referral program work?">
    Refer a friend and you collect **100% of the trading fees on their first 100 trades**, credited to your balance in real time. See [Referral Program](/platform/referrals).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I'm a public figure. How do I claim my NPSI profile?">
    Visit your profile on pauv.com and select **Claim Your Profile**. Our team confirms your identity, and you begin receiving a share of the trading activity on your profile. See [For Public Figures](/platform/for-talent).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get a public figure listed?">
    Anyone can suggest a public figure. Pauv reviews and decides before the profile goes live. See [For Public Figures](/platform/for-talent).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
