## 5. Forecasts and DTM 4.1

- **Forecasts.** A forecast is a directional view on an NPSI expressed through Pauv's proprietary pricing engine, **DTM 4.1**, a single-curve bonding model with a softplus price floor. A **Positive Forecast** (long) pulls the NPSI up; a **Negative Forecast** (escrow-backed short) pulls it down.
- **Negative Forecast liquidation.** Negative Forecasts are fully collateralized by an on-platform escrow equal to twice the user's net stake. A Negative Forecast is automatically closed if the cost to buy back its tokens reaches the position's liquidation threshold; at that point the user's return is zero. The liquidation threshold is shown on the position before it is opened and may tighten at low NPSI prices for book-stability reasons. A user cannot lose more than the net stake committed to a Negative Forecast, but may lose all of it in a sharp adverse move of the NPSI.
- **No counterparty matching.** All forecasts settle instantly against DTM 4.1. There is no order book, no bid/ask, no secondary market, and no user-to-user matching.
- **No ownership.** Opening a forecast does not give you ownership of the NPSI, the public figure, or any enterprise. The NPSI is a signal, not an asset.
- **No borrowing; no leverage.** Negative Forecasts do not borrow tokens or funds from other users. Both legs of the escrow originate from the user's net stake and capital extracted from the curve itself. Pauv does not offer margin, lending, or leveraged forecasts.
- **Internal positions.** DTM 4.1 uses internal token accounting, but users do not directly interact with tokens. Positions are platform records; they are not cryptocurrency, not transferable, and have no value outside the Platform.
- **Algorithmic pricing.** NPSI values are determined solely by forecasting activity through the published DTM 4.1 algorithm. The starting values of the original 101 NPSI profiles were set through Elo-based ranking by beta users; new profiles are seeded through Elo-Based Comparative Pricing.

## 8. Fees

- **Standard fee.** Every forecast (open or close) incurs a total fee of **1.8%** on the gross cash amount: **1.3%** to Pauv and **0.5%** to a good-faith royalty set aside for the public figure associated with the NPSI. - **Fee isolation.** Fees are collected into a separate treasury ledger and do not enter the bonding curve. Fees do not affect the NPSI value or pricing math.
- **Round-trip cost.** A user who opens and immediately closes a forecast pays approximately 3.57% in total fees, the compounded effect of paying 1.8% on both legs.
