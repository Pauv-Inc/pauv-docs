## Withdrawals vs. Refunds

Pauv is a **fully liquid, closed-loop platform**. Because you can close your forecasts against DTM 4.1 at any time and withdraw your on-platform balance to your connected Solana wallet, Pauv does not offer traditional "refunds" for Platform activity, NPSI-driven losses, or automatic liquidations of Negative Forecasts.

If you wish to retrieve your funds, simply close your active forecasts to restore your balance and initiate a withdrawal through the Platform.

<Note>
  **Beta withdrawal review.** While we ensure the stability of our trading engine, every withdrawal request is reviewed manually and approved (or denied for a genuine error) within 12 hours. A denial only happens when our review finds a real flaw — for example, if the trading engine credited a balance amount it should not have. If a withdrawal is denied, we will contact you to explain and resolve it.
</Note>

## No "Change of Mind" Refunds

We do **not** offer refunds or reversals for:

- Changing your mind after opening a Positive or Negative Forecast
- NPSI movements that result in a loss when you close your forecast
- Automatic liquidation of a Negative Forecast when its buy-back cost reaches the position's liquidation threshold
- Fees paid on opening or closing a forecast
- Dissatisfaction with Platform features
- Standard withdrawal review timing (up to 12 hours during beta)
- USDC sent in the wrong token, on a network other than Solana, or to an address other than your Pauv deposit address
- Funds lost due to a compromised or misconfigured wallet

## Exceptions (Bona Fide Errors)

Pauv Inc. may, at its sole discretion, correct your on-platform balance or process a re-credit in the following specific scenarios:

| Scenario | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Deposit Not Credited** | Your USDC deposit was confirmed on-chain to your Pauv deposit address but never credited to your balance due to a system failure. |
| **Engine Error** | A bug in the trading engine resulted in your balance being calculated incorrectly. This may go in either direction: an under-credit is restored, and an over-credit is the basis for denying a related withdrawal during beta review. |
| **Unauthorized Activity** | We verify that activity on your account was the result of unauthorized access to your Pauv account (subject to investigation). |

## How to Report an Error

To report a Bona Fide Error, email **support@pauv.com** within **7 days** of the transaction. Include:

1. Your account email
2. The on-chain transaction signature (for deposit issues) or the affected forecast / position ID
3. A clear description of what you expected vs. what happened
