## Withdrawals vs. Refunds

Pauv is a **fully liquid platform**. Because you can close your positions against VBC 4.1 at any time and withdraw your balance, Pauv does not offer traditional "refunds" for Platform activity, NPSI-driven losses, or automatic liquidations of shorts.

If you wish to retrieve your funds, simply close your active positions to restore your balance and initiate a withdrawal through the Platform. Withdrawals are processed automatically, with no manual review.

## No "Change of Mind" Refunds

We do **not** offer refunds or reversals for:

- Changing your mind after opening a long or short position
- NPSI movements that result in a loss when you close a trade
- Automatic liquidation of a short when its buy-back cost reaches 95% of the position's escrow
- Fees paid on opening or closing a trade
- Dissatisfaction with Platform features

## Exceptions (Bona Fide Errors)

Pauv Inc. may, at its sole discretion, correct your balance or process a re-credit in the following specific scenarios:

| Scenario | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Deposit Not Credited** | Your Glide deposit completed but was never credited to your balance due to a system failure. |
| **Engine Error** | A bug in the pricing engine resulted in your balance being calculated incorrectly. This may go in either direction: an under-credit is restored, and an over-credit is corrected on the balance. |
| **Unauthorized Activity** | We verify that activity on your account was the result of unauthorized access to your Pauv account (subject to investigation). |

## How to Report an Error

To report a Bona Fide Error, email **support@pauv.com** within **7 days** of the transaction. Include:

1. Your account email
2. The Glide transaction reference (for deposit issues) or the affected trade or position ID
3. A clear description of what you expected vs. what happened
