*Last Updated: May 18, 2026*

Pauv Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share personal information when you visit pauv.com (the "Platform") or use our services. By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect three categories of information from users of the Platform.

### A. Voluntary Data (Information You Provide)

- **Account Registration:** Your name and email address.
- **Connected Wallet:** The public address of any Solana wallet you connect to your account, used to credit crypto deposits and to send crypto withdrawals.
- **Verification Data (KYC):** If you make a fiat deposit, government ID details, date of birth, and address — collected securely by our verification partner **Veriff** through the Soap Payments gateway. Verification is required for all fiat deposits; crypto deposits do not require it.
- **Claim Flow (Public Figures):** If you claim an NPSI profile to direct your good-faith royalty, we collect information sufficient for our team to confirm that you are the individual represented by the profile, plus the payout destination you choose (a Solana wallet address or a 501(c)(3) designation).
- **Support:** Information you send when you contact customer support.

### B. Automatic Data (Information Collected via Technology)

- **Device & Usage:** IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and timestamps.
- **Activity:** Pages viewed, time spent on the Platform, and interface interactions.
- **Cookies:** We use cookies and local storage to maintain your login session and ensure Platform security.

### C. Transaction Data

- **On-Platform History:** Records of deposits, withdrawals, balance, forecasts opened and closed, and good-faith royalty disbursements.
- **On-Chain Data:** Solana transactions to and from your connected wallet that interact with the Platform are recorded on the public Solana ledger. Pauv does not control public-ledger data.

## 2. Public Figures and Public-Domain Information

NPSI profiles are derived data products reflecting public sentiment about cultural figures. Pauv does not collect, store, or use a public figure's personal information for the purpose of listing their NPSI.

- **No personal data required to list an NPSI.** NPSI profiles exist because public sentiment is public domain.
- **No biometric data.** Pauv does not store or use biometric data of indexed individuals.
- **Claim-based data collection.** If a public figure chooses to claim their profile, we collect the verification information and payout details described in Section 1.A at that point, solely to direct the good-faith royalty.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Service Delivery.** To create and maintain your account, credit deposits, process withdrawals, settle forecasts, and disburse good-faith royalties.
- **Integrity & Safety.** To detect manipulation, abuse, account takeovers, and other harm to the Platform or its users.
- **Communication.** To send transaction confirmations, security alerts, and administrative updates.
- **Service Providers.** We share necessary data with our payment and verification partners — **Soap Payments** (payment gateway), **Glide** (crypto processing), **AptPay** (fiat payments and FBO custody), and **Veriff** (identity verification for fiat deposits) — solely to provide services and meet legal obligations. These partners process your data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

## 4. Your Privacy Rights

Pauv extends the following rights to all users, consistent with the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA) and other applicable state privacy laws:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | Request a copy of the personal data we maintain about you. |
| **Correction** | Request that we correct inaccuracies in your personal data. |
| **Deletion** | Request that we delete personal data you have provided (records we are legally required to retain, such as KYC records, are excluded). |
| **Portability** | Request a copy of your data in a digital, machine-readable format. |
| **Appeal** | Appeal our decision if we deny your request. |

**Other state privacy laws.** If you reside in a state with its own comprehensive privacy law — including, without limitation, California (CCPA/CPRA), Virginia (VCDPA), Colorado (CPA), Connecticut (CTDPA), and Utah (UCPA) — you may exercise any additional rights granted by the law of your state of residence, such as the right to opt out of targeted advertising, the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data, or profiling in furtherance of decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. Pauv does not sell personal data. Verified requests are honored in accordance with the law applicable to you.

**To exercise your rights:** Email **support@pauv.com**. We will authenticate your identity and respond within 45 days (or any shorter period required by the law of your state).

## 5. Data Security & Retention

- **Security.** We implement industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for data in transit and at rest.
- **Retention.** Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Platform is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18 who attempt to open an account. Pauv does not list NPSI profiles for individuals under 18 — only adult public figures are indexed.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
