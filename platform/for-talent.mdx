## Why Your NPSI Exists

Every public figure on Pauv has a **Net Public Sentiment Index (NPSI)**: a live, dollar-denominated measure of their cultural relevance. The NPSI exists because **public sentiment is public domain**. How the world perceives a cultural figure is not owned by that figure, unlike their Name, Image, or Likeness.

Pauv does not ask public figures to "agree to be listed." Listing an NPSI is not a commercial use of identity; it is indexing the public's reaction to it. The NPSI is a derived data product, similar to a bond rating or a credit score.

<Note>
  Every profile carries a clear non-endorsement disclaimer: *"[Name] is not affiliated with Pauv and has not endorsed, sponsored, or authorized this NPSI listing. All imagery is copyright-free or AI-generated."*
</Note>

## Listing a Public Figure

Listings on Pauv are approval-gated:

<Card title="Suggest a figure" icon="user-plus">
  Any user can suggest a public figure to be listed. Pauv reviews the suggestion and decides whether to list.
</Card>

We do not list private individuals or individuals under 18. A profile is only listed once Pauv has reviewed the request.

## Claim Your Profile

Every NPSI profile features a prominent **"Claim Your Profile"** button. When you claim your profile, Pauv confirms your identity, and from the moment your claim is approved you receive a share of the trading activity on your profile going forward. Nothing accrues before you claim.

## What Claiming Is — and Is Not

Claiming your profile **only** determines whether you receive a share of trading activity on it going forward. It is not an endorsement, partnership, or authorization.

- Claiming **does not** constitute endorsement of, affiliation with, or authorization of Pauv.
- Claiming **does not** grant Pauv any rights to your Name, Image, or Likeness.
- Claiming **does not** create a partnership, sponsorship, or business relationship.
- Claiming **does not** give you control over the NPSI value, trading behavior, or the presentation of your profile.

The NPSI is driven entirely by trading activity on the platform. Public figures have no role in setting, managing, or supporting the value.

## How to Claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your profile">
    Visit your NPSI profile on pauv.com and select **Claim Your Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identity check">
    Pauv reviews each claim manually to confirm you are the individual represented by the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share begins on approval">
    Once your claim is approved, your share of trading activity on your profile begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## No Minors

Pauv does not list NPSI profiles for individuals under 18. Only adult public figures are indexed on the Platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign anything for my NPSI to exist?">
    No. NPSI profiles exist because public sentiment is public domain. No consent is required for a public figure to be indexed, and no one at Pauv "signs" or "partners with" any individual to list their NPSI.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I take my NPSI down?">
    No. The NPSI is a derived data product reflecting public sentiment and is not subject to removal on request. You can, however, claim your profile at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does claiming make me a Pauv partner or endorser?">
    No. Every NPSI profile explicitly states that the public figure is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Pauv.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do I get if I claim?">
    A share of the trading activity on your profile, starting from the point your claim is approved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
