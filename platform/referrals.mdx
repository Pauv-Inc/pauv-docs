## How It Works

Refer a friend to Pauv and you collect **100% of the trading fees on their first 100 trades**, credited to your balance **in real time**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite a friend">
    Share your referral link from your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They sign up and trade">
    Your friend creates an account through your link and starts trading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You collect their fees">
    For each of their first 100 trades, the full trading fee on that trade is credited to your balance the moment the trade settles.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Details

- Every leg counts: each open and each close your friend makes is a trade.
- Credits land in your balance in real time — no waiting period, no minimum payout.
- Referral credits are part of your balance: use them to trade or withdraw them at any time.
