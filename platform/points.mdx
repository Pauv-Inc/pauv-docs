## Your On-Platform Balance

Your **balance** is the dollar-denominated number you see in the app. It tracks the value you have available to open forecasts.

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Display** | Dollars ($). 1 USDC deposited = $1 of on-platform balance |
| **Funded by** | USDC on Solana, sent from your connected wallet |
| **Transferable to other users?** | No |
| **Withdrawable to your wallet?** | Yes, in USDC on Solana |

## Connecting Your Wallet

Pauv runs on **Solana**. To deposit or withdraw, connect a Solana wallet to your account:

- **Browser extension** — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any other Solana-compatible wallet.
- **Mobile** — scan the QR code displayed in the app from your mobile wallet.

Your wallet address is the address Pauv will send your withdrawals to. Make sure the wallet you connect is one you control.

## Depositing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deposit screen">
    From your account, choose **Deposit**. The app will display your Pauv deposit address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send USDC on Solana">
    From your connected wallet, send **USDC on Solana** to that address. Only USDC on Solana is supported — sending any other token, or USDC on a different network, may result in loss of funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance credited">
    Once the deposit is confirmed on-chain, your on-platform balance is credited dollar-for-dollar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Your Balance

All forecasting on Pauv happens against your on-platform balance:

- **Open a forecast.** Commit a dollar amount to a public figure's NPSI as a Positive Forecast (long) or Negative Forecast (escrow-backed short).
- **Close a forecast.** The curve calculates your proceeds and credits them back to your balance instantly.
- **Instant settlement.** Because your balance is an on-platform record, forecasts open and close immediately without waiting on the chain.

## Withdrawing

You can withdraw your balance back to your connected wallet at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a withdrawal">
    From your account, choose **Withdraw** and enter the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manual review (beta)">
    During the beta period, every withdrawal is reviewed by our team. Reviews complete within **12 hours**. The only reason a withdrawal would be denied is a genuine flaw or error — for example, the trading engine credited you more than it should have. If that happens, we will contact you to explain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="USDC sent to your wallet">
    Once approved, USDC is sent to your connected Solana wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Withdrawals are sent only to the Solana wallet that is currently connected to your account. To withdraw to a different wallet, connect that wallet first.
</Note>
