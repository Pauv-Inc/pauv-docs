## Your Balance

Your **balance** is the dollar-denominated number you see in the app. It tracks the value you have available to trade.

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Display** | U.S. dollars ($), credited 1:1 with your deposits |
| **Funded by** | Glide |
| **Transferable to other users?** | No |
| **Withdrawable?** | Yes — automatically, at any time |

## Depositing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Deposit">
    From your account, choose **Deposit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay through Glide">
    Complete your payment through **Glide**, our payment partner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance credited">
    Your balance is credited in U.S. dollars, 1:1 with your deposit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Your Balance

All trading on Pauv happens against your balance:

- **Open a position.** Commit a dollar amount to a public figure's NPSI — long (up) or escrow-backed short (down).
- **Close a position.** The curve calculates your proceeds and credits them back to your balance instantly.
- **Instant settlement.** Because your balance is an on-platform record, trades open and close immediately.

## Withdrawing

You can withdraw your balance at any time. Withdrawals are **automatic** — there is no manual review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a withdrawal">
    From your account, choose **Withdraw** and enter the amount.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processed automatically">
    Your withdrawal is processed automatically, with no manual review.
  </Step>
</Steps>
