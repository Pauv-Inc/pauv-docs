## Your On-Platform Balance

Your **balance** is the dollar-denominated number you see in the app. It tracks the value you have available to open forecasts.

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Display** | Dollars ($). Deposits are credited at their U.S. dollar value |
| **Funded by** | Crypto (any token, any network) or fiat (card / bank transfer) |
| **Transferable to other users?** | No |
| **Withdrawable?** | Yes — as USDC on Solana to your connected wallet, or as a fiat payout |

## Payment Partners

All funding runs through our payment gateway, **Soap Payments**:

- **Glide** processes crypto deposits — any token, on any network.
- **AptPay** processes fiat payments; fiat funds are held in a regulated **FBO custodial account**.
- **Veriff** performs identity verification (KYC), required for all fiat deposits.

## Connecting Your Wallet (Crypto)

To deposit or withdraw crypto, connect a Solana wallet to your account:

- **Browser extension** — Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, or any other Solana-compatible wallet.
- **Mobile** — scan the QR code displayed in the app from your mobile wallet.

Your wallet address is the address Pauv will send your crypto withdrawals to. Make sure the wallet you connect is one you control.

## Depositing Crypto

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the deposit screen">
    From your account, choose **Deposit** and select crypto. The app will display your Pauv deposit address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send any token, on any network">
    Send crypto to that address from your wallet. Deposits are processed through the Soap Payments gateway via **Glide**, which accepts any token on any network. No identity verification is required for crypto deposits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance credited">
    Once the deposit is confirmed on-chain, your on-platform balance is credited at the deposit's U.S. dollar value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Depositing Fiat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    All fiat deposits require identity verification (KYC), performed by **Veriff** through the Soap Payments gateway. You complete this once, before your first fiat deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit by card or bank">
    From your account, choose **Deposit** and select a fiat method. Payments are processed by **AptPay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Balance credited">
    Your funds are held in a regulated FBO custodial account and your on-platform balance is credited dollar-for-dollar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Your Balance

All forecasting on Pauv happens against your on-platform balance:

- **Open a forecast.** Commit a dollar amount to a public figure's NPSI as a Positive Forecast or an escrow-backed Negative Forecast.
- **Close a forecast.** The curve calculates your proceeds and credits them back to your balance instantly.
- **Instant settlement.** Because your balance is an on-platform record, forecasts open and close immediately without waiting on the chain.

## Withdrawing

You can withdraw your balance at any time. Withdrawals are **automatic** — there is no manual review.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a withdrawal">
    From your account, choose **Withdraw**, enter the amount, and pick your payout method — USDC on Solana to your connected wallet, or a fiat payout via AptPay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Processed automatically">
    Your withdrawal is processed immediately, with no manual review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds arrive">
    Arrival time depends on the payout method you selected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Crypto withdrawals are sent only to the Solana wallet that is currently connected to your account. To withdraw to a different wallet, connect that wallet first.
</Note>
