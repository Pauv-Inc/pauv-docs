## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [pauv.com](https://pauv.com) and create your account with your **name** and **email address**. That is all we collect at sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Solana wallet (for crypto)">
    Connect a wallet from your browser via extension (e.g. Phantom, Solflare, Backpack), or scan the QR code displayed in the app to connect from a mobile wallet.

    Your wallet address is linked to your account and is used for crypto deposits and crypto withdrawals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your account">
    Fund with **crypto or fiat** — your on-platform balance is credited at the U.S. dollar value of your deposit:

    - **Crypto:** Deposit **any token on any network**. Crypto deposits are processed through our Soap Payments gateway via **Glide** and credited once confirmed on-chain. No identity verification required.
    - **Fiat:** Deposit by card or bank transfer, processed by **AptPay** and held in a regulated FBO custodial account. All fiat deposits require identity verification (KYC), performed by **Veriff** through our Soap Payments gateway.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your first forecast">
    Browse NPSI profiles, form a read, and open a **Positive Forecast** (you think cultural relevance will rise) or a **Negative Forecast** (you think it will fall). Every forecast settles instantly against VBC 4.1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Eligibility

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Age** | Must be 18 or older to open a forecast |
| **Residency** | United States only |
| **Wallet** | A Solana wallet (e.g. Phantom, Solflare, Backpack) — required for crypto deposits and crypto withdrawals |
| **Verification** | Identity verification via Veriff — required for all fiat deposits |

## Account Security

Your wallet is the source of truth for who can deposit to and withdraw from your account. Treat your wallet's recovery phrase the way you would treat the keys to your bank account — Pauv cannot recover funds lost to a compromised wallet.

If you suspect unauthorized access to your **Pauv account** (not your wallet):

1. Email **support@pauv.com** immediately with "URGENT: ACCOUNT SECURITY" in the subject line
2. Your account will be temporarily frozen to prevent unauthorized activity
3. Our team will work with you to secure your account

## Tips for New Users

- **Start small.** Familiarize yourself with the platform and VBC 4.1 before committing larger amounts.
- **Understand the model.** The NPSI moves based on forecasting activity. Read [How It Works](/how-it-works) to understand VBC 4.1 and fees.
- **Negative Forecasts can be liquidated.** An escrow-backed Negative Forecast is automatically closed at zero return if the NPSI rises enough that the buy-back cost reaches the position's liquidation threshold. You cannot lose more than your net stake, but you can lose all of it in a sharp adverse move.
- **Forecasts are for entertainment.** Opening a forecast is a way to express conviction about public perception. Do not commit funds you cannot afford to lose.
- **Withdrawals are automatic.** Press withdraw and funds arrive in a timeframe that depends on the payout method you choose. See [Funds](/platform/funds).
