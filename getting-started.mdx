## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Visit [pauv.com](https://pauv.com) and create your account with your **name** and **email address**. That is all we collect at sign-up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your account">
    Choose **Deposit** and pay through **Glide**. Your balance is credited in U.S. dollars.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place your first trade">
    Browse profiles and trade **up** or **down** on someone's Sentiment. Every trade settles instantly against VBC 4.1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Account Security

If you suspect unauthorized access to your Pauv account:

1. Email **support@pauv.com** immediately with "URGENT: ACCOUNT SECURITY" in the subject line
2. Your account will be temporarily frozen to prevent unauthorized activity
3. Our team will work with you to secure your account

## Tips for New Users

- **Start small.** Familiarize yourself with the platform and VBC 4.1 before committing larger amounts.
- **Understand the model.** The NPSI moves based on trading activity. Read [How It Works](/how-it-works) to understand VBC 4.1 and fees.
- **Shorts can be liquidated.** An escrow-backed short is automatically closed if the NPSI rises enough that the buy-back cost reaches 95% of the position's escrow. You receive whatever remains of the escrow; you cannot lose more than your net stake, but you can lose nearly all of it in a sharp adverse move.
- **Withdrawals are automatic.** Press withdraw and it processes with no manual review. See [Funds](/platform/funds).
